Accounting services

  • Retainer services
  • Auditing
  • Preparation of financial statements
  • Filing of BIR returns and other government reportorial requirements (ITR/AFS)
  • Bookkeeping of financial transactions

Documentation services

  • DTI/SEC registration
  • Business permit (Mayor’s permit)
  • BIR registration
  • SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG registration
  • PCAB license assistance
  • Tax clearance
  • PhilGEPS (Platinum certificate)

Services by agency

Business registration, business closure, audited financial statements, tax filing and compliance, tax clearance, transfer of business address and BIR tax assessment (LOA).

DTI

Business name registration, initial registration, amendments, renewal and closure.

SEC

Business name registration, initial registration, amendments, renewal and closure.

PCAB

License assistance for new licenses, renewals and upgrading.

PhilGEPS

Registration for red membership, platinum upgrade and platinum renewal.
